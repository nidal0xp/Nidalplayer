Nidalplayer v4.0

Privacy Policy • Last updated: August 2026

Summary: Nidalplayer is a privacy-first, client-side media player. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal information. All your data stays strictly on your local device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Nidalplayer does not operate any user tracking servers or telemetry systems. We do not collect:

  • Personal identification details (name, email address, phone number).
  • Device identifiers or unique advertising IDs.
  • IP addresses or geographic location data.
  • Usage analytics, browsing history, or playback metrics.

2. Local Storage and Data Retention

All application settings and playlist information are stored locally on your device using local storage or protected application directories. This includes:

  • Playlists & Credentials: Stream URLs, usernames, and passwords you provide to connect to your media providers.
  • User Preferences: Selected UI language, playback engine choice, and volume levels.
  • Watch Progress & Favorites: Marked favorite channels and resume timestamps for movies and series.

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any third party.

3. Direct Stream Connections

When you stream media content, Nidalplayer establishes a direct client-to-server connection between your device and the media provider URL you specified. Nidalplayer does not proxy or intermediate your media streams.

4. App Permissions

The application only requests permissions strictly necessary for core functionality:

  • Internet Access: Required to connect to your specified media stream providers and fetch content.
  • Local Wi-Fi Network Access: Used exclusively to enable the optional local phone remote control feature on your home Wi-Fi network.
  • Wake Lock: Prevents your screen from sleeping during active video playback.

5. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual playlists or perform a complete wipe of all locally saved credentials and watch history at any time through the in-app Settings → Reset All Data option.

6. Contact & Updates

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your application distribution channel.
